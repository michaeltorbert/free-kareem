About

Semper Fi, then ship it.

Marine Corps veteran. College dropout. Founder of the most popular SEO plugin in WordPress history. Those three facts explain almost everything else.

🪖

I'm Michael Torbert. I served in the United States Marine Corps, which taught me more about discipline, ownership, and getting things done than any classroom ever did — which is convenient, because I dropped out of college.

In 2007 I took over a small WordPress plugin called All in One SEO Pack. It grew into the most downloaded SEO plugin in WordPress history, with millions of active installs. To support it I founded Semper Fi Web Design and later Semper Plugins, home of the Pro version and our premium plugin lineup.

Along the way I co-authored WordPress All-in-One For Dummies, hosted and sponsored WordCamp Raleigh, and organized the local WordPress Meetup — where you'll still find me talking about SEO, Apache directives, and using WordPress as a CMS.

When I'm not shipping code, I'm usually behind a camera or on a plane. My wife and I have made it to seven countries and fifteen states so far, and we're not done.
