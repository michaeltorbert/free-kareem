Read this before buying from Kimi's Carpets Plus in Fuquay Varina

MT
Michael Torbert First-hand customer review · Updated regularly

The short version: in my experience, Kimi's Carpets Plus in Fuquay Varina overpromises and underdelivers — and accepts payment for work they're not able to complete. This is my honest, first-hand account so you can make an informed decision before hiring them. Nothing here is hearsay; it all happened to my family.

The project

We hired Kimi's Carpets Plus — owned by Mike and Kimberly Quinn — to replace all the carpet in our house with hard-surface flooring: luxury vinyl plank (LVP) throughout. This was a $12,000+ project, paid for in good faith, scheduled well in advance.

What went wrong

  • The project was delayed at the last minute by two weeks, after we had already prepared the house.
  • The work was left incomplete, and in my experience they attempted to collect as if it were finished.
  • When problems were raised, they refused to acknowledge their mistakes.
  • The owner was, in my experience, rude and unprofessional, with a temper directed at both employees and customers.

The response to this review

Rather than making things right, they hired a lawyer to send me a letter threatening a lawsuit over my negative review. I stand by every word on this page: it is my honest opinion and my family's first-hand experience, which is exactly what consumer reviews are for.

A business that responds to honest criticism with legal threats instead of solutions is telling you everything you need to know.

Separately: Kimberly Quinn is an admin of the local "Ladies of Fuquay Varina" Facebook group, and after my wife shared her honest review there, she was blocked from the group.

Frequently asked questions

Would you hire Kimi's Carpets Plus again?

No. Based on my experience, I'd encourage anyone in Fuquay-Varina, Holly Springs, Apex, or Cary to get quotes elsewhere and read recent reviews carefully first.

Is this review still accurate?

Yes. I keep this page up to date, and nothing about my experience has changed. If the company ever makes things right, I'll say so here.

Why publish this on your personal site?

Because review platforms can be pressured, but my own website can't be. This page exists so my neighbors can read the full story before spending five figures.
