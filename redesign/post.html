All in One SEO Pack — updated

MT
Michael Torbert 4 min read · Filed under WordPress

A new version of All in One SEO Pack is out today. This release focuses on something I've been asked about constantly at Meetups and WordCamps: using WordPress as a full content management system, not just a blog — and making sure your SEO holds up when you do.

What's new

  • Expanded support for custom post types and static front pages, so CMS-style sites get the same fine-grained control as posts.
  • Smarter canonical URLs to keep duplicate content from diluting your rankings.
  • Assorted bug fixes reported by the community — thank you, as always, for the detailed reports.
  • Security hardening throughout the options handling.
The best SEO plugin is the one you can set up once and trust to do the right thing on every page you publish.

Upgrading

As always, update from your WordPress dashboard or grab the latest from the plugin repository. Settings carry over automatically. If you've customized rewrite_titles or your canonical settings, take thirty seconds to confirm they look right after upgrading.

Pro users: the corresponding Pro release is live at SemperPlugins.com with the same fixes plus this cycle's premium features.

Thank you

This plugin exists because of the people who use it, report bugs, translate it, and tell their friends. If it's helped your site get found, leave a review — or come say hi at the next WordPress Raleigh Meetup.
